**The first release of Mz808**, an emulation of the Roland TR-808 Rhythm Composer as a VST3 instrument and a standalone app.

![The Mz808 panel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simozzer/Mz808/v0.1.0/docs/screenshot.png)

### Download

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Mz808-0.1.0-win64-Setup.exe` | **The installer.** Installs the VST3, the standalone app or both, for everyone or just you, with an uninstaller. Run it, then rescan plugins in your DAW. |
| `Mz808-0.1.0-win64-VST3.zip` | The plugin, to install by hand. Copy `Mz808.vst3` to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Common\VST3` (or `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3`) and rescan in your DAW. |
| `Mz808-0.1.0-win64-Standalone.zip` | `Mz808.exe`, which plays without a DAW. |

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The installer and binaries aren't code-signed, so Windows may warn about an unknown publisher. Choose *More info → Run anyway*, or build from source.

### What's in it
Every sound is modelled on the 808's circuits rather than sampled:
- **Bass drum:** from a published analysis of the circuit. It has the punchy attack, the pitch "sigh" of a long decay, and accents that bloom rather than just get louder.
- **Metal:** the cymbal, hihats and cowbell share six oscillators that never stop, so no two cymbal hits are the same.
- **Accent as the 808 does it:** a harder strike into the circuits, not a volume boost.
- **The shared circuits:** a conga cuts off its tom, claves cut off the rim shot, maracas cut off the clap, and a closed hihat chokes an open one.

All sixteen sounds, with every knob from the original panel:
- bass drum, snare
- low, mid and hi toms and congas
- rim shot and claves, hand clap and maracas
- cow bell, cymbal, open and closed hihat

**Playing it:**
- **MIDI:** notes follow the General MIDI drum map, and velocity above 100 is an accent.
- **Pads:** sixteen pads on the panel play each sound and light up whenever it is struck.
- **MIDI control:** every knob has a MIDI CC, and you can MIDI-learn your own.
- **Nine factory kits,** and hover hints on every control.

### Known limitations
- **No sequencer yet.** Program patterns in your DAW, or play the sounds from a pad controller.
- **One output.** The hardware has an output per instrument; Mz808 mixes them to one for now.
- **Windows only** for now.
- **Not calibrated against a real TR-808.** The bass drum and cymbal follow published circuit analyses, and the oscillator frequencies are published figures. Other ring times, filters and levels are worked out from the circuits, not measured from a unit.
